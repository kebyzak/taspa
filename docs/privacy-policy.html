Taspa Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Taspa is an app for tracking and rating TV shows and movies. This policy explains what data Taspa collects, why, and how you can delete it.

Data we collect

  • Account data: your email address, password (stored only as a secure hash), username and user ID, needed to create and sign in to your account.
  • Profile photo: if you choose to upload an avatar, the image is stored on our servers and shown on your profile.
  • Activity in the app: the titles you track, your ratings, reviews, lists, follows and other social activity, so the app can show your library and profile to you and to other users.
  • Imported data: if you import ratings from another service, the file you choose is processed to add those ratings to your library.
  • Device token: if you allow notifications, a push token from Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can send you notifications.

Taspa does not show ads, does not use advertising or analytics trackers, and does not sell your data.

How we use your data

We use your data only to provide the app's features: your account, library, ratings, profile, social features and notifications.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase hosts our database, authentication and file storage.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers push notifications.
  • TMDB provides show and movie information. Our server requests catalog data from TMDB; your personal data is not sent to it.

These providers process data on our behalf. We do not share your personal data with anyone else, except where required by law.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to your private data is limited to your account.

Public information

Your username, profile photo, ratings and reviews may be visible to other Taspa users.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Account → Delete account. Your account, profile, photo, library, ratings, reviews and device tokens are then permanently deleted, usually within a few minutes. See how to delete your account.

Children

Taspa is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
