Delete your Taspa account

In the app

  1. Open Taspa and sign in.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and enter your current password to confirm.

Without the app

Email kazbek.auez@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Taspa account". We will delete your account within 7 days.

What is deleted

Your account and email, username, profile photo, library, ratings, reviews, lists, follows, notifications and device tokens are permanently deleted. When you delete in the app this usually happens within a few minutes. Deleted data cannot be restored.

What is kept

Nothing linked to your account is kept after deletion. Backups held by our hosting provider expire on their normal schedule.
