# Taspa logo concepts

[Concept board](taspa-logo-concepts.png) · Selection checkpoint

Taspa is a cinematic diary for movie and TV viewers. The mark should feel precise, restrained, and editorial. These studies use the approved graphite `#141416`, ivory `#F3EEE8`, and fired coral `#DB806F`. Both the symbols and the wordmark lettering are drawn as vector paths. The lettering takes its tone from the app's approved typography.

| Direction | Construction | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Splice**, recommended | A single T with two parallel 45° cuts suggests the angled splice of a strip of tape. The wordmark repeats the cut in its initial. [Symbol](vectors/splice-symbol.svg) · [Wordmark](vectors/splice-wordmark.svg) | The initial is familiar. The two cuts do the work of making it recognizable as Taspa's. |
| **Match Cut** | Two offset frame corners leave a shared aperture. A geometric wordmark carries their clarity into the name. [Symbol](vectors/match-cut-symbol.svg) · [Wordmark](vectors/match-cut-wordmark.svg) | The film-editing idea is indirect and can read as a framing tool in isolation. |
| **Colophon** | A drawn serif wordmark lends the diary the voice of a small publisher. Its lowercase a becomes the standalone signature. [Symbol](vectors/colophon-symbol.svg) · [Wordmark](vectors/colophon-wordmark.svg) | It has the closest editorial character. The initial link to Taspa is weaker and the letter detail is subtler at 16 px. |

**Why Splice.** It gives Taspa one shape that can carry the name from the wordmark to the home screen. The tape reference is visible in the cut, the T remains readable in one color, and the serif display typography can retain its existing role in the app.

### Preview checks

The board includes actual 64, 32, and 16 px symbol samples, black and reversed white tests, coral on graphite, and rounded-square and circular treatments. The symbols were also reviewed rotated and mirrored, and compared beside four media marks from the design reference library. The SVGs use solid fills and outlines with no embedded fonts or images.

The icon treatments are concept previews. Platform-ready artwork follows selection, with square layers for Apple's system mask and separately prepared Android foreground, background, and monochrome layers within the adaptive-icon safe area. Device rendering and trademark clearance remain open.
